Questions owners ask

Do I have to replace ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro?

No. Those tools run your trucks, your invoices and your dispatch. We sit in front of them and make sure the call turns into a booked job. Everything we book is pushed into your existing software.

What happens at day 30?

We send you a one-page summary of what was recovered and booked. If you want to continue, it's $997 a month from then on and nothing else changes. If not, we switch it off and remove it. Either way, nothing is owed for the trial.

Does the text sound like a robot?

You saw an example at the top of the page. The messages are written for your company and the AI keeps the conversation short and practical. If a caller asks something it can't handle, it hands off to you with a text so you can jump in.

What do you need from me to get started?

About 20 minutes on a call, a phone forwarding setting, access to your calendar, and an export of your past customers for the first campaign. Most shops are live within a week.

Why is the first month free?

Because it's the fastest way for both of us to find out if this is worth your money. Owners don't want a sales pitch, they want to see what showed up on the calendar. We'd rather show you than tell you.

Will this work if we're already busy?

Busy is exactly when calls get missed. The system fills gaps in your calendar, chases open estimates you've forgotten about, and books your past customers for maintenance before the season rush. It doesn't create work for your office.
